# The Atlan challenge brief

Manan is doing a GTM work sample for Atlan: "Find the first 100 teams for Atlan Agent Registry."

**Objective:** choose one ICP and one primary distribution channel, defend the pairing, execute a first campaign that is ready to run (not future tense), and ship a working artifact.

**Four deliverables:**
1. Decision doc/deck — ICP, channel bet, campaign, distribution loop, activation definition, next experiments.
2. The thing built — runnable and inspectable.
3. Evidence appendix — sources, discovery notes, assumptions, rejected alternatives.
4. An AI work log — approach, reusable systems built, one place AI was wrong, and the decision AI could not make.

**Format:** the core submission should be understandable in 15 minutes. The review session is 50 minutes — 20 readout/demo, 20 pushback, 10 changing one part live. Atlan budgets 5-7 calendar days from receipt to schedule the session.

**Operating rules:** confidence tags (`[verified]` / `[reported]` / `[assumption]`) on every claim, hypotheses written before evidence with kill criteria, null results logged, load-bearing facts independently verified, and a blocked source retried through another surface before being written down as "nothing found."
