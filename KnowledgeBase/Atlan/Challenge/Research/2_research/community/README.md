## The five buckets
- **B1 — the sharing moment.** Person A getting a skill to person B. The arrow the GTM bet depends on.
- **B2 — sprawl and decay.** Too many skills, wrong skill firing, context budget, duplicates, staleness.
- **B3 — trust.** Running someone else's skill, prompt injection, secrets, review before adoption.
- **B4 — counter-evidence.** Commissioned deliberately. The strongest honest case for doing nothing.
- **B5 — signal shape.** Who talks, at what volume, and whether a channel exists at all.
