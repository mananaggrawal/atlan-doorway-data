# Work logs
Every log for this project, in one place. Written as the work happened, not reconstructed.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `ai_work_log.md` | **Deliverable 4.** Approach to AI, models and harnesses used, reusable systems built, where AI was wrong, the decision AI could not make. The polished export lands in `submission/4_ai_work_log/`. |
| `research_run_log.md` | Every research run: purpose, model, method, tool calls, duration, output files, headline finding, and what failed. The audit trail behind `2_research/`. |
| `access_and_blockers.md` | Every source that could not be reached, what was tried, and how it was resolved or worked around. Blocked is not the same as absent. |
| `session_log.md` | Chronological record of decisions and turning points. |
