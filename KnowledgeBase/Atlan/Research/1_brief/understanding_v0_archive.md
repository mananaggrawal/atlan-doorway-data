## What's in it
- Problem/solution framing of the Registry ("GitHub for agent skills and context", desktop app): auto-discover → single source of truth → discover & reuse → govern → distribute → observe → improve.
- Objective restated: path to first 100 teams; who, why, how to reach, what repeatable motion.
- ICP hypotheses (broad): Data & Analytics, AI/ML, Engineering, IT/Platform, and business teams (sales, marketing, content, legal, ops).
- Six success criteria: ICP & wedge, channel bet, execution, evidence & learning, path to 100, AI work log.
- ~30 clarification questions grouped as: definition of "team", activation & metrics, product current state, existing signal & ICP, GTM & execution surfaces, strategic thesis (skills as wedge), what "great" looks like.

## How we use it
1. The clarification list is good raw material but too long to send as-is. Cut to the 5-6 questions whose answers would actually change what we build; answer the rest ourselves and state the assumption in the deck. Asking 30 questions in week one reads as unwilling to decide.
2. The ICP list is a list, not a hypothesis. The brief warns explicitly: *"A list of plausible channels is not a strategy."* Same applies to ICPs. We narrow to one, with a trigger.
3. The success criteria are close to right and worth keeping as the spine of the final deck.
