THREE THINGS THAT CHANGED THE PICTURE:
1. Truncation is SOLVED FREE (docs + /doctor + /skill-doctor + configurable budget, May-Sep 2026).
   Leading with "your skills are silently disappearing" would get us corrected.
2. Broadcasting is strong; DIRECTED HAND-OFF is what's absent. The demand is not "help me share" -
   it is "tell me which one is approved and let me get it without asking a human."
3. 23 skill registries launched Dec 2025-Sep 2026 and scored ~47 HN points combined. One "shared
   brain for your team" scored 93 alone. Do not build a destination; build a by-product.
