# Primary research: Manan's own interviews
Recorded 2026-09-08. THIS IS THE ONLY PRIMARY, NON-PUBLIC EVIDENCE IN THE SUBMISSION.
Everything else is a bet on public information (per the Atlan CEO: the Agent Registry is used only
internally, there is no customer base and no good-quality demand signal from it yet).

n = 4 conversations, plus Manan's own experience as a sales / pre-sales professional.
- 2 x tech sales (tech-savvy)
- 1 x non-tech sales
- 1 x performance-marketing user
Sampling is convenience-based and small. Label it as such - it is directional colour, not a survey.

## TECH SALES / SALES ENABLEMENT  [stated by interviewees]
THE STACK: HubSpot (CRM) + email + LinkedIn + Google Drive + Apollo. Wired together into one system
that automates the entire outbound motion.
WHAT THEY BUILT AS SKILLS:
- find the right customers by reading signals from public forums
- reach out to those people via LinkedIn and email
- follow up
- enrich from Apollo where required
- send DMs, draft emails
THE CRUX FINDING: there is a SPECIFIC VERBIAGE that works for one demographic of buyers. The sales
user encodes that verbiage as a skill. THAT SKILL ONLY WORKS FOR THAT PERSON.
The persona is typically a SALES ENABLEMENT professional.
THREE THINGS THEY CANNOT DO TODAY:
1. extend the skill to other users on the team
2. let the org protect the skill if that person leaves
3. (implied) know whether anyone else is getting value from it

## PERFORMANCE MARKETING  [stated by interviewee]
Job: shell out a LARGE, REPEATED volume of performance creatives - static and video.
Built skills in Claude, hooked up the Higgsfield MCP to generate the creatives.
NO CONSISTENCY ACROSS THE ORG - each user built their own skills, or Claude built them for them.
(Matches the public null result: this segment builds heavily but privately and publishes nothing.)

## THE CONSTRAINT MANAN ADDED, AND IT IS LOAD-BEARING  [stated 2026-09-08]
These users sat in orgs that LET PEOPLE CHOOSE THEIR OWN AGENTS - i.e. NOT on a centrally-procured
enterprise plan from a single vendor. Typically STARTUPS.
WHY THIS MATTERS: BYO-agent is the precondition for the sprawl. If IT hands everyone one harness and
one governed skill library, there is nothing to register. The sprawl exists precisely BECAUSE
procurement never happened. Corroborated publicly by Menlo: sales AI is 78% startup-share, bought
bottom-up outside IT.
This also means the ICP filter is NOT "big enterprise with governance budget" - it is the opposite:
"company big enough to have the mess, small enough that nobody was ever put in charge of it."
