# Prospects
`prospect_candidates.md` — named people who publicly expressed the pain, harvested by every research run as a by-product of the evidence sweep. Now sorted into three tiers (see the top of that file) rather than one flat list.

`atlan_prospects_68.xlsx` — the same 68 people as one spreadsheet: name/handle, tier, platform, what they said or did, link, and notes. Filterable by the Tier column. Built 2026-09-05 for outreach planning. (Binary spreadsheet — not mirrored here; the same data is in `prospect_candidates.md`.)

Columns on the raw rows: handle · where · what they said · link · bucket · reachability.

Roughly 70 candidates across GitHub, Hacker News, X, LinkedIn and Reddit, now read as three groups:
- **Tier 1 — highest intent:** people who already built or are maintaining a homegrown fix for a slice of this problem (sync, governance/audit, manual usage measurement) — revealed preference, not stated preference. See `hypotheses.md` H10. This is the first outreach priority.
- **Tier 2 — documented pain:** the authors of the duplicate GitHub issues asking for team skill sharing. Several wrote multi-paragraph technical specs, which means they will talk — but this is stated pain, one rung below Tier 1.
- **Tier 3 — leverage, not prospects:** founders already building point solutions as public products (Tessl, Ingot, OzBrain, shareskills.ai, sx, Askill, noriskillsets.dev, setoku, skillrecall, AgentSeal). Competitive intelligence and, in some cases, partnership or press — never pitched as customers, since they're rivals, not the ICP.

This doubles as the campaign's first audience list. Any outreach states plainly that it is a candidate exercise, per the brief.
