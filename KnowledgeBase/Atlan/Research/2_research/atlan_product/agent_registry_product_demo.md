## Description

See how Atlan helps teams discover, govern, and distribute AI skills and agents across Codex, Claude Code, and other tools.

This demo covers:
- Personal and department workspaces
- Reporting for usage, spend, adoption, and ROI
- Agent 360 for dependencies, models, users, and traces
- Skill discovery, versioning, duplication, usage, and security
- Distribution of governed skills into the tools teams already use

Demo data is used throughout. Some security capabilities shown are in development.
