The build

Atlan Pulse

npx atlan-pulse

Reads your skills and your session history. Tells you what's dead, duplicated, expensive, unowned or over-permissioned.

Never invokedhas never once run
Concentrationtop 3 skills' share
Context costwhat it costs per session
Staleold, still loaded
Orphanedno owner
Duplicatessame job, different words
Risky permswider access than needed
Packbundle + send to a teammate

The three in blue need session history. Competitors scan files — history is the only thing that can say a skill is dead, not just old. Until it's on npm, npx github:mananaggrawal/atlan-pulse is the form that runs today.
