# Decision deck — storyline and structure

The decision deck is Deliverable 1: a published HTML deck (not PowerPoint), 18 slides, light theme.

**Density rule:** first draft was ~120 words/slide and was rejected as too dense. Now ~30 words/slide — the idea, not the argument for the idea. The defence lives in the evidence appendix and in narration.

**Storyline (YC-style arc):** research → problem → hypothesis of pain → competition → value → solution → distribution → math. Framing corrections made along the way: never frame findings as "we killed our own hypothesis" — instead "Three cohorts. One complaint underneath" (the team, the power user, the reviewer — each with a verbatim sourced quote). Never "we assumed the wedge was open" — instead "Everyone is building the same shelf. So the wedge has to be somewhere else."

**The math closer:** "100 teams needs 1,700 scans. Here's where they come from." Outbound 8 + r/claudeskills 400 + Show HN 500 + the GitHub-issue comment 250 + awesome-lists 200 + SEO 350 = 1,708 solo scans → ×12% = 205 two-person workspaces → ×50% = 102 activated teams. The 12% conversion rate is flagged as the one number that can't be fully defended.

**Structure (original 16, now 18):** cover · research · three cohorts · the deepest pain · the saturated shelf · crowded-vs-open · Atlan Pulse · the report (screenshot) · the page (screenshot) · the loop · why anyone shares · reach vs activation · who · where · what we said no to · the math to 100 — plus two inserted slides after Research: "ICP identification" (fourteen personas tested, one clears the bar) and "The decision" (the firm that bills for a repeatable deliverable, with the $4,000/mo verified quote).

**Known internal tension, flagged not yet resolved:** most of the deck (cohorts, who, where, the math-to-100 model) still reflects the pre-reframe engineer-ICP framing, built before the 08 Sep HR/CEO reframe. Only the two new ICP slides were added per explicit scope; a larger rewrite around the agency/consultancy ICP is a deliberate pending decision, not an oversight.

**The share mechanic** ("you share the score, you never share the skill"): a rendered card (`npx atlan-pulse --card`) combining a confession ("14 of my 41 have never run"), a percentile (only Pulse has the denominator), and a depth credential (volume proves you're early, zero disclosure). This same mechanic generalizes cleanly to the agency ICP's client-facing version without needing to be reinvented.
