# Reframe after HR + CEO feedback (2026-09-08)

Atlan HR and the CEO reviewed the in-progress submission and gave feedback that reset the direction.

**HR's read:** the submission deviated from the assignment's actual purpose — a crystal-clear ICP and a campaign with an installation flow / distribution loop. "The solution came too early": Atlan Pulse arrived before the ICP work justified it.

**CEO's read:** Atlan's own Agent Registry is used only internally today, with no customer base and no reliable demand signal from it — so the whole exercise is a bet built on public information, and research is the right instrument. Usage of a product like this is driven by people doing things extremely unique to their company — marketing, sales, pre-sales, not just coding. Coding has the deepest AI penetration today, then voice — so AI-native orgs matter most. The objective is the most dramatic distribution possible. Overall direction was judged good.

## Constraints set afterward

- No deadline — optimize for the quality of the bet, not speed.
- One primary ICP plus an expansion map, not two co-equal ICPs.
- Atlan Pulse's fate is deferred until the ICP work lands.
- The ICP must be crystallised deeply: tools used today, software, team size.
- The ICP sits in **BYO-agent orgs** — people choosing their own agents rather than a centrally-procured enterprise plan — typically startups.
- Authorised forward assumptions: models keep improving; AI needs to go multiplayer.
- No further primary interviews — the existing n=4 stands.

## The n=4 primary research (the only non-public evidence in hand)

2 tech sales, 1 non-tech sales, 1 performance marketer, plus Manan's own sales/pre-sales background.

- The tech sales interviewee had built HubSpot + email + LinkedIn + Drive + Apollo into an automated outbound system with skills for signal-based prospecting, outreach, follow-up and enrichment. The crux: a specific verbiage that works for one buyer demographic gets encoded as a skill and only works for that one person — it can't extend to the team, and the org can't protect it if they leave.
- The marketer ships high volumes of static and video creative via Claude + Higgsfield MCP with no consistency across the org.
- Manan's own hunch going in — "users are asking to share skills, but I am not convinced yet" — was proven right by later research: the sharing hypothesis did not hold up.

**How to apply:** don't re-defend the old bet. The three live questions are which ICP is primary, what replaces sharing as the wedge, and which distribution loop survives the constraint that the underlying object is proprietary.
