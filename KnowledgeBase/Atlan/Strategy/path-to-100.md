# Path to 100 — the funnel model

The funnel: install → solo scan → invite one teammate → second person scans into the same workspace → a third person plus one skill gets a named owner, version, and reuse = activated team (the D1 activation bar).

**Supply:** 58 real direct-outreach prospects (6 Tier 1 highest-intent who already built their own fix; 52 Tier 2 documented-pain), plus 10 Tier 3 competitors explicitly excluded from outreach. Plus one r/claudeskills post (~66K members) as the highest-variance lever in the model.

**Wave 1 arithmetic** (every rate tagged `[assumption]` — none is measured yet): direct outreach yields ~8 solo scans; the Reddit post yields ~160-1,080 (the biggest unknown); combined ~170-1,090 solo scans. A 12% solo-to-second-person conversion (anchored to PLG invite benchmarks, pulled down by the "never train your replacement" Reddit finding) yields ~25 two-person workspaces; a 50% rate to full 3-person activation yields ~6-40 activated teams from Wave 1 alone — deliberately not 100.

**Why that's fine:** the brief's own stated assumption is "a validated, evidenced path to 100 — not 100 signups during the exercise." The path to 100 is Wave 1 *repeating*: renewable Tier 2 supply (new GitHub issues keep appearing), the aggregate "State of Agent Skills" report becoming its own second-wave acquisition asset once enough runs accumulate, second-wave subreddits (r/ClaudeCode, r/ClaudeAI) opening once the tool is proven rather than pitched cold, and activated-team members re-triggering the same solo-scan mechanic at their next company.

**Most sensitive number:** the solo-to-second-person conversion rate (Stage 2) is the single least-validated assumption in the whole model.
