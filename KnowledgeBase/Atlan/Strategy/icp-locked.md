# ICP — locked, 2026-09-08

This supersedes the earlier "firm that bills for a repeatable deliverable" framing, which is now a secondary monetization story only (not the primary build target).

**One sentence:** a solo builder or very small team who has published their own working Claude Skills as a public GitHub repo for reasons unrelated to Atlan — audience, credibility, teaching — and who now has forks and strangers depending on a repo they maintain alone.

**Profile:** 1-10 people, no IT/procurement, BYO-agent by construction (pays for Claude personally or on a startup card). Uses Claude Code/Cowork daily to do their actual job — shipping software, client work, content. The public repo is content marketing for their real business (a course, consulting, a newsletter, a personal brand), not altruism. They're most reachable when the repo has real traction or is about to be published, because that's when its credibility is top of mind.

**Two concrete examples:**
1. The indie technical educator — shaped like `mattpocock/skills` (257k stars, 19k forks): a solo dev/educator monetizing a course or consulting practice separately.
2. The solo growth/marketing consultant — shaped like `coreyhaines31/marketingskills` (48k stars, 6.9k forks, still climbing): building a personal brand feeding a newsletter/community. This sub-segment answers the CEO's marketing/sales-persona signal without needing a second ICP.

**Why it's locked:**
- The population is counted, not estimated: 257k / 211k / 48k stars and 19k / 6.9k / 69 forks across three named repos, pulled live.
- The distribution loop is observed, not analogized: shields.io serves 1.2M READMEs and 1B+ requests/month — forks copy the badge mechanically.
- BYO-agent is definitional, and the ICP spans both engineering and marketing/sales without needing a split.

**Ruled out:** legal/HR (checked, empty). Agencies — the strongest pain signal ($4k/mo quote from a real thread) — but that loop is borrowed by analogy from Clutch/G2, and a funded competitor (agentman.ai) already ships the exact gating mechanic.
